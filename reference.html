QCFlows shows how correlations build up between qubits as a circuit runs. Build a circuit gate by gate, press Run, and watch pairwise measures (K, Entanglement of Formation, Mutual Information) appear as a graph, a matrix, and per-state amplitudes.

Pair metrics

The graph and matrix show one selected pair metric at a time. Missing entries mean that no pair density matrix is available for that marker.

Metric

K metric

K is directional and basis-specific. Ki→j is high when measuring qubit i in the selected basis prepares distinguishable conditional states on qubit j.

K metric paper

Entanglement of Formation

EoF is a symmetric two-qubit entanglement measure. It is zero for separable pairs and one for a maximally entangled Bell pair.

Short background

Mutual Information

MI is a symmetric total-correlation measure. It includes both classical and quantum correlations and is reported in bits.

Short background

Qubit nodes

Each circle in the graph is a qubit. Its color shows where its single-qubit state sits in the chosen measurement basis — the same color scale the graph uses.
